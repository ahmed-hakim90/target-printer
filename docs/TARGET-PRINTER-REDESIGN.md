# Target Printer redesign decision

- Target outcome: a complete, conversion-led product website for Modern Egypt's Target printing brand.
- Source of truth: public company content and media from `modernegypt.com.eg`; the supplied screenshot is visual direction, not a pixel-perfect clone.
- Information architecture: Home → Products → Product detail → Solutions / Spare parts → Quote or direct contact.
- Brand direction: deep Target blue, white product canvases, Cairo typography across Arabic and English, restrained elevation, real company assets only.
- Responsive rule: desktop uses product grids and a split hero; mobile reprioritizes to one column with persistent access to WhatsApp.
- Home hero: the factory photograph is a full-bleed responsive background with the official Target mark, directional contrast overlays, and mobile focal-position adjustments so the content remains readable without duplicating a separate product-card visual.
- Scope decision: all existing routes are enabled; inquiry submission intentionally opens the user's email client while WhatsApp remains an immediate alternative.
- Localization: English and Arabic share a persisted language context; Arabic switches the document to RTL and both languages use Cairo. Directional icons mirror logically.
- Motion: every route section enters through one IntersectionObserver-based motion system, with reduced-motion support. Route-level skeletons cover navigation loading and product grids.
- Social links: the footer links to Modern Egypt's confirmed Instagram, Facebook and YouTube profiles. Each external profile opens in a separate tab with an accessible name; WhatsApp, email and telephone remain available through the site's contact actions.
- Spare-parts scope: retained because the source company explicitly supplies genuine printer and photocopier parts; copy is constrained to inks, drums, rollers, print heads, electrical parts and maintenance kits rather than unrelated industrial components.
- Testimonials: all available reviews render in a looping, drag-enabled carousel. Autoplay pauses during pointer, hover or keyboard interaction, resumes afterward, and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Catalog normalization: the 27 source records resolve to 19 unique product models. Duplicate posts map to one canonical detail page while their distinct images and non-conflicting specifications are retained.
- Header identity: the navigation uses Modern Egypt's official blue logo asset. The former white source variant was not reused because it disappeared against the light header; no substitute Target logo is shown.
- Search discovery: `sitemap.xml` is generated from the public routes and normalized catalog during every production build, while `robots.txt` permits public crawling, excludes API paths and advertises the sitemap. Canonical and Open Graph URLs use the production origin.
- Performance and access: the above-the-fold hero is resized and compressed from roughly 1 MB to 223 KB, the browser receives explicit image dimensions and high fetch priority, and stable vendor chunks improve repeat-visit caching. A keyboard skip link and pressed states for catalog filters supplement the existing focus and reduced-motion system.
- Spare-part details: each of the six supported spare-part groups has a canonical detail route with bilingual overview, applications, compatibility checks, service scope, related parts and quote/WhatsApp actions. The legacy source only confirms the general supply of genuine printer and photocopier parts, so compatibility is explicitly subject to model, serial number and technician verification; unverified stock codes are not invented.
- Printed-catalog review: the 18-page December 2025 company catalog was checked through both text extraction and rendered-page inspection. Its confirmed upcoming products (Nail Printer, Coffee Printer and Portable Printer), four consumable series and production-machine highlights are represented in bilingual site sections. Where a copied office-printer table conflicts with the more specific live product record, the site keeps the existing model record instead of silently replacing it with contradictory catalog copy.
- Horizontal-motion system: categories, differentiators and business solutions use one native continuous rail built from three identical segments. It starts on the middle segment and normalizes its scroll position by one exact segment, preventing the RTL overlap and empty-space failures caused by per-slide loop repositioning. Products keep snap navigation, while desktop testimonials rotate three fixed grid positions without cloned slides. All rails pause during interaction, restart after 900ms and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Product cards surface up to three category-relevant specifications from the canonical product data. Office printers prioritize speed, duplex and connectivity; production equipment prioritizes speed, working width and ink configuration; finishing equipment prioritizes working width, speed and operation. The strip is informational and preserves the existing detail and quote journeys.
- Driver download: every product detail page exposes the same bilingual, direct-download action for the versioned Modern Egypt printer setup package. The installer is served as a static public asset and its release filename is preserved for customer support and cache clarity.
- Supplied product photography: the 21-product public catalog comprises the 18 active products and the three explicitly labelled upcoming products in the December 2025 PDF. Every catalog entry uses its extracted local product image; confirmed model-labelled uploads extend the TA-P3300DNW, TA.M3300 and TA-P2000W detail galleries. Exact duplicate uploads are stored only once, and the unmatched TA-M2500DNW set is held back rather than assigned by visual similarity.
- Category product discovery: the home page includes a second, distinct catalog experience after the metrics. Accessible category tabs update a large product presentation and a compact draggable rail using the normalized 19-model catalog; the one model without source specifications is explicitly marked as available on request.
- Ambient motion: deterministic, randomly distributed dots move behind translucent page surfaces rather than above controls or text. Mobile density is reduced and motion is disabled when the visitor requests reduced motion.
